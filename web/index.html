    Welcome to meshscan

    Four quick questions and your Cardputer is a mesh node. You can change all of this later in Settings.

    Where are you?

    Nodes only hear each other on the same radio settings, and those are agreed per region.

    Pick a PIN

    Anyone on your network can watch the dashboard. Sending messages, switching modes and changing settings need this PIN.

    How should it start?

    You can switch any time from the header.

    ChatJoin the mesh: read and send on the Public channel. Transmits when you send.
    ScanListen only. Hops between MeshCore, Meshtastic and LoRaWAN presets and maps what's around.
    Home location for the radar (optional)
    Lets the Cardputer radar place nodes by real bearing and distance.

    Unlock

    Enter the PIN to send, switch modes and change settings.

    Settings

    Saving restarts the radio (about 10 seconds).
